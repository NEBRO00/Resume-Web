Since 2016

Built by three roasters who couldn't agree on one bean.

Ember & Oak started as a shared roaster in the back of a bike shop. We couldn't settle on a single "house" coffee, so we decided not to — every batch is small enough to change, and every menu is a snapshot of what tasted best in cupping that week.

Today we still roast on-site, five days a week, and buy directly from three growing partners we visit every year.

How a bag gets to your cup

01

Source

Green beans bought direct from partner farms in Ethiopia, Colombia and Guatemala.

02

Roast

Five-kilo batches, roasted on-site every morning and rested for 48 hours.

03

Cup

Every batch is tasted blind by the whole team before it goes on the menu.

04

Pour

Brewed to order at the bar — nothing sits in a pot waiting for you.

Direct trade

We pay above fair-trade average and visit our three growing partners every year.

Zero waste bar

Spent grounds go to a community garden two blocks away. Milk comes in returnable bottles.

Neighborhood first

Free cupping nights on the last Thursday of every month — open to anyone curious.
